# Oral exam: causal inference without package incantations

You should be able to answer each in under 90 seconds.

1. Why can adding controls increase omitted-variable bias?
2. What exactly does a firm fixed effect remove? What does it not remove?
3. Derive the 2x2 DiD estimand from four cell means.
4. Why can staggered TWFE use already-treated units as controls?
5. What does a lead coefficient in an event study actually test?
6. Why does an insignificant pre-trend test not establish parallel trends?
7. State all four core IV assumptions and explain LATE.
8. What happens to 2SLS with weak instruments?
9. What is the continuity assumption in RDD?
10. Why inspect the running-variable density around the cutoff?
11. Why is synthetic control fundamentally a weighting problem?
12. What does common support mean in propensity-score matching?
13. Why can balance improve while identification remains bad?
14. When should standard errors be clustered, and at what level?
15. What is treatment-effect heterogeneity and why does it matter for aggregation?
16. Distinguish adoption, exposure, intent-to-treat, and treatment-on-the-treated.
17. Explain interference and why firm-level AI adoption may violate SUTVA.
18. What is a negative-control outcome?
19. What does a placebo treatment date diagnose?
20. When should you refuse to call an estimate causal?
